Texas heat, pollen, and hard water put extra stress on swimming pools. Homeowners in Spring often ask how frequently a professional should visit.

## Weekly Service Is Ideal During Swim Season

From late spring through early fall, **weekly pool cleaning** is the gold standard for most residential pools in the 77379 area. Here's why:

- Chlorine residual drops faster in high temperatures
- Debris and pollen accumulate quickly
- Filter pressure rises with heavier use
- Early detection of equipment issues prevents costly failures

A weekly visit typically includes skimming, brushing, vacuuming, emptying baskets, testing and balancing chemicals, and a quick equipment check.

## Bi-Weekly Can Work in Cooler Months

When water temperatures drop and swimming slows, many Spring homeowners switch to **every other week**. Chemical demand decreases, but you still need regular testing to avoid algae blooms when the weather warms again.

## What Happens If You Skip Service?

Skipping two or more weeks often leads to:

- Cloudy water that requires clarifiers or floc
- Green algae that needs shock treatment and multiple recovery visits
- Higher repair costs for pumps and filters that ran under stress

The cost of a recovery service almost always exceeds the price of consistent weekly maintenance.
