When the pool pump stops, circulation ends and water quality declines within days. Here are the most common causes we see on service calls in Spring and Klein.

## 1. Failed Start Capacitor

The capacitor gives the motor the jolt it needs to start. When it fails, the pump hums but does not turn. This is one of the quickest and least expensive repairs.

## 2. Worn Shaft Seal

A leaking seal lets water reach the motor bearings. You may notice water dripping under the pump or a grinding noise. Replacing the seal early prevents motor failure.

## 3. Seized or Burned-Out Motor

Overheating, age, or running dry can kill the motor. Variable-speed pumps are more efficient but still need proper voltage and clean baskets.

## 4. Clogged Impeller or Basket

Leaves, toys, and debris can block flow. Always check the skimmer and pump baskets first—many “dead” pumps simply need cleaning.

## 5. Power or Timer Issues

Tripped breakers, bad timers, or loose wiring are surprisingly common. We always verify power before replacing parts.
